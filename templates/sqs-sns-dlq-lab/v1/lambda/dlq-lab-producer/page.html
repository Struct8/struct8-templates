SNS, SQS and dead-letter queue lab

Each message published here goes to the orders topic.

Send a message

The orders topic delivers a copy of each message to Track 1, Track 2a and Track 2b. Each track retries in its own way and has its own dead-letter queue. The behavior field of the message tells the consumers to succeed or to fail.

Where to look

  • Dead-letter queues: the SQS console.
  • The alarm on the Track 1 dead-letter queue: CloudWatch alarms.
  • What each consumer did: its log, from the Lambda console.

A message stays in a dead-letter queue until it is deleted. The alarm stays in ALARM while the Track 1 dead-letter queue holds any message.

Track 4 publishes a message that succeeds every minute, so each consumer also runs once a minute with no click here.
