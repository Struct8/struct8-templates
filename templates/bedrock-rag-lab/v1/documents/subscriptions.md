# Bean Lab Coffee — Coffee subscriptions

## Plans

| Plan | What arrives | Price per delivery |
|---|---|---|
| Single origin | One 340 g bag of a different single-origin coffee | US$ 19 |
| House blend | One 1 kg bag of the house espresso blend | US$ 38 |
| Discovery | Three 115 g bags from three countries | US$ 24 |

Deliveries go out every 2 weeks or every 4 weeks, as chosen at sign-up.
Subscription shipping inside the United States is always free.

## Billing

A subscription is charged 2 days before each delivery leaves the roastery. The
receipt arrives by email on the day of the charge.

## Pausing and cancelling

A subscription can be paused for up to 3 deliveries in a row, or cancelled at
any time, from the account page. A change made less than 2 days before the next
charge applies from the delivery after it.
