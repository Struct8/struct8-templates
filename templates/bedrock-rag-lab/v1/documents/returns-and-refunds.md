# Bean Lab Coffee — Returns and refunds

## Coffee

Unopened bags of coffee can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Opened bags
cannot be returned, except when the coffee arrived stale: in that case the bag
is replaced, and no return is needed.

## Equipment

Grinders, kettles and brewers can be returned within 60 days of delivery, in
their original packaging. Equipment carries a 2-year warranty against
manufacturing defects. Damage from descaling with vinegar is not covered.

## How to return

1. Write to the support team at support@example.com with the order number.
2. The team answers within 2 business days with a prepaid return label.
3. Ship the item within 14 days of receiving the label.

## Refunds

The refund goes to the original payment method within 5 business days of the
return arriving at the roastery. Shipping costs are refunded only when the item
was defective or the wrong item was sent.
