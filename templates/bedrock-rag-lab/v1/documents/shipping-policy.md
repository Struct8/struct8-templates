# Bean Lab Coffee — Shipping policy

Bean Lab Coffee is a fictional coffee roaster used in this lab. Every order ships
from the roastery in Portland, Oregon.

## When an order leaves the roastery

Coffee is roasted on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays. An order placed before
10:00 Pacific time on a roasting day ships the same day. Any other order ships
on the next roasting day.

## Delivery times

| Destination | Delivery time after shipping | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| United States | 2 to 4 business days | Free on orders over US$ 35, otherwise US$ 6 |
| Canada | 5 to 7 business days | US$ 15 |
| Mexico | 7 to 10 business days | US$ 18 |

Bean Lab Coffee does not ship to any other country.

## Tracking

Every order gets a tracking number by email when it leaves the roastery. The
tracking page updates within 24 hours of shipping.

## Lost or damaged packages

A package that has not arrived 10 business days after the end of its delivery
time counts as lost. A lost or damaged package is replaced at no cost: write to
the support team with the order number and, for a damaged package, a photo.
